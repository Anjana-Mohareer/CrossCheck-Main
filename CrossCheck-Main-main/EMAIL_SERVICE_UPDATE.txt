CrossCheck Email Service Update

- Added Spring Boot Mail dependency.
- Sends a welcome email to every newly registered Admin, HR or Recruiter account.
- Sends candidate update emails to the candidate's respective email address and the logged-in user's registered email address.
- Added emails for candidate creation, profile revision, verification changes, offer-letter review, hiring and document upload.
- Added forgot-password request email.
- Added configurable Gmail/SMTP support using environment variables.
- Email errors are logged and do not interrupt candidate operations.
- Email preview mode is used by default until SMTP is enabled.
